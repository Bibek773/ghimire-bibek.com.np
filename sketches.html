How Commissions Work

A simple five-step process from enquiry to finished artwork

1
Send a Reference Photo

Share a clear, well-lit photo of the subject. Better reference means a better result.

2
Discuss the Details

We'll talk about size, style, level of detail, background preference, and your deadline.

3
Confirm & Agree

Once both sides are happy with the plan, we confirm the commission and finalise the terms.

4
Sketching Begins

I start working on the portrait. Progress updates can be shared during the process.

5
Final Delivery

The finished artwork is photographed, shared digitally, and delivered in person or by post.

Pricing Information

Pricing is flexible and discussed for each commission individually. I don't use a rigid fixed rate — instead, pricing is based on what your specific project actually needs.

  • Paper size
  • Number of faces
  • Level of detail
  • Turnaround time
  • Delivery method

Starting from approximately Rs 3,500/- for a standard single-face portrait on A4 paper. Reach out via email or Instagram to get a quote for your specific request.
